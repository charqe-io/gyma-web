Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Türkçe

This English version is a translation provided for convenience. The Turkish original is the legally binding text; in the event of any discrepancy, the Turkish version prevails.

This Privacy Policy explains how your personal data is collected, used, shared and protected in connection with the GymA mobile app, connected wearable device apps and the gyma.charqe.io website (the "Services") provided by Charqe Bilişim Teknolojileri Sanayi Ticaret Limited Şirketi ("Charqe", "we").

In short: We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. By default, your workout, nutrition and measurement data are visible only to you. What you share on the social side, however, is visible to other users unless you choose otherwise. Location sharing is off by default and works only when you turn it on. We never see your payment details.

3. What Do We Use Data For?

  • To create your account, verify your identity and keep you signed in;
  • To provide core features such as workout logging, cardio tracking, statistics, personal record detection and calorie estimates;
  • To run nutrition, water and supplement tracking and to show summaries based on your goals;
  • To record your body measurements and chart how they change over time;
  • To operate the social features: showing posts and stories to the audience you choose, establishing follow relationships, delivering messages and publishing events;
  • If you have turned it on, to show nearby athletes and events;
  • To create your weekly and program-based summary reports and, depending on your preference, send them to you by email;
  • To send personal record, reminder and social interaction notifications (subject to your permission);
  • To verify your subscription status and manage access to paid features;
  • To review complaints, enforce the Community Guidelines and detect abuse and fake accounts;
  • To keep the Services secure, diagnose and fix errors and improve the App;
  • To fulfill our legal obligations and respond to requests from competent authorities.

We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing purposes. The App contains no third-party advertising networks, analytics SDKs or tracking tools. We do not use your data for profiling that produces automated legal effects concerning you.

5. Location Data

Location data has a special place in the App, so we explain how it works separately:

  • It is off by default. Your location is processed only when you have both granted device permission and explicitly enabled location sharing within the App.
  • We do not track you continuously. Your location is updated only while the App is open in the foreground, at intervals of at least five minutes. We do not collect your location in the background and do not keep any route or movement history.
  • Only your latest location is stored. No historical record of your location is kept in the database; each update overwrites the previous one.
  • It is shown to other users as a distance. In the "nearby athletes" list, only an approximate distance is shared, not your exact coordinates.
  • Stale locations drop out. Locations that have not been updated for a certain period are excluded from nearby user queries.
  • You can turn it off. When you turn off Settings > Privacy > Show me nearby, your location is not used in discovery features. When you delete your account, your location record is deleted as well.
  • Story location tags and event locations are information you add knowingly, and they are visible to the users who see that content.

6. Payment Data

Paid features are sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment details such as your card number, expiry date, CVV and billing address are processed directly by the relevant store operator; they are not passed to us and we do not store them. We only receive information on whether your subscription is valid, which package you use and its validity period.

7. Where Data Is Stored and Retention Periods

Your data is stored by our infrastructure provider Supabase on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany) region.

Data Retention period
Account, profile, workout, nutrition and measurement data As long as your account is active; deleted or anonymized when you delete your account
Data of a frozen account 30 days from the date of freezing. If you sign in within this period, the account is restored as it was; if you do not, the account and all data are deleted automatically
Posts and comments Until you delete them or close your account
Stories Removed from the feed 24 hours after posting; media files are cleaned up within a reasonable time
Direct messages Until one of the conversation participants deletes them or closes their account
Location information Only your latest location is kept; it is cleared when you turn sharing off or delete your account
Smartwatch pairing records Valid for a few minutes; consumed once used
Notification tokens As long as the device remains connected and notification permission is on
Moderation and complaint records For as long as the review and any legal claims require
Invoice and transaction records For the period required by tax and commercial legislation (as a rule, 10 years)

You have two options for closing your account. Freezing signs you out and keeps your data as it is; if you sign in again within 30 days, your account is restored immediately, and if you do not, the account and all data are deleted automatically. Permanent deletion deletes your account and data without any waiting period. For your security, both actions are verified with your password.

When you delete your account, your personal data is deleted or irreversibly anonymized within a reasonable time. However, content in the conversation records of other users you have messaged, data that must be retained due to a legal obligation or an ongoing claim, and copies that remain in technical backups for a reasonable period may fall outside the scope of this deletion. Deleting your account does not automatically cancel your app store subscription.

  • In response to lawful requests from competent public institutions and organizations under applicable legislation;

  • In other cases where you have given your explicit consent.

10. International Data Transfers

Since your data is hosted on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany) region, this processing constitutes a cross-border transfer within the meaning of Article 9 of Law No. 6698. The transfer is carried out on the basis of the appropriate safeguards provided for in the legislation (standard contractual clauses, a letter of undertaking or other applicable transfer conditions) or, where necessary, your explicit consent. For details, see the KVKK Information Notice.

11. Data Security

Your data is encrypted in transit with TLS. Database access is limited by Row Level Security rules to the data associated with your account only. Passwords are stored in irreversibly hashed form; not even we can see your password. Smartwatch pairing records are valid for only a few minutes and can be used only once. Access permissions are limited to the minimum level needed to do the job.

Direct messages are not end-to-end encrypted; they are stored on our servers to provide the service, ensure security and fulfill legal obligations. Please remember that no system is 100% secure; we recommend choosing a strong password for your account that you do not use for any other service. In the event of a security breach affecting your personal data, we will inform you and the Personal Data Protection Board within the time and in the manner prescribed by the legislation.

12. Your Rights

Under Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK) and related legislation, you have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to object to processing, to withdraw your explicit consent and to learn which third parties your data has been transferred to. For the full list of rights and the application procedure, see the KVKK Information Notice.

Controls you can use directly within the App:

  • Editing your profile and goal information;
  • Deleting or archiving your posts, stories and comments;
  • Making your account private, blocking and restricting users, and managing your close friends list;
  • Turning off location sharing, gym visibility, workout schedule visibility and event invitations;
  • Managing your notification and weekly email report preferences;
  • Freezing your account, or permanently deleting your account and data.

You can send your requests to info@charqe.io.

13. Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed at users under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. The accounts and data of users found to be under 18 are deleted. If you believe your child has provided us with data, contact us at info@charqe.io.

14. Cookies

This promotional website does not use cookies and contains no third-party analytics or tracking tools. The mobile app uses only local session and settings information stored on your device to keep you signed in and remember your preferences; this information is not used for advertising or profiling.
